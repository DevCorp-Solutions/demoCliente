## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the sensory theatre of an artisanal wood-fired asador and premium steakhouse. It balances primeval culinary elements—smoldering holm oak coals, searing embers, deep char, and rich marbling—with refined, contemporary editorial restraint. The visual language conveys quiet culinary mastery, patience, authenticity, and unpretentious luxury.

The target audience comprises gastronomic enthusiasts, meat connoisseurs, and diners seeking an authentic sensory ritual rooted in tradition, natural wood fires, and dry-aged cuts. The interface avoids aggressive commercial clutter, favoring ample breathing room, literary typography, subtle textural warmth, and tactile clarity. The design style combines **Minimalist Editorial Luxury** with **Warm Tactile Gastronomy**, employing warm bone tones by day, deep charcoal ember darkness for focal experiences, and measured terracotta flame accents.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the stages of a charcoal grill: the cold bone of linen tablecloths, the deep soot of aged cast iron, the radiant amber glow of living embers, and the terracotta rust of open flames.

- **Primary (`#9C382C` & `#B83E2D`):** Terracotta Rust / Ember Flame. Used for primary calls to action (reservations), key accent dividers, active states, and flame insignia motifs.
- **Secondary (`#14100E`):** Deep Charcoal Carbon. Used for high-contrast hero narrative blocks, primary typography on light surfaces, and grounding footer structures.
- **Tertiary (`#D49B53`):** Aged Hearth Gold. Used sparingly for curated accolades, italics within hero statements, star ratings, and vintage date stamps.
- **Neutral (`#FBF7F2` with container tone `#F5EFE6`):** Warm Bone / Cream. Forms the foundational canvas for daytime reading, menus, and item cards, eliminating harsh sterile whites in favor of an organic parchment warmth.
- **Hairline Border (`#E6DFD5`):** Subtle sand tone for card framing, metadata dividers, and ticker ribbons without breaking visual flow.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the literary dignity of **Playfair Display** with the sharp, functional precision of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Headlines & Expressive Quotes:** Playfair Display introduces editorial elegance. Italic variants in aged gold (`#D49B53`) within charcoal canvases evoke culinary passion and time-honored craft.
- **Micro-Kickers & Eyebrows:** Rendered in small, wide-tracked uppercase Plus Jakarta Sans (`letter-spacing: 0.18em`) paired with horizontal hairline rules (`—`) to establish culinary sections like "DIRECTO DE LA BRASA" or origin markers.
- **Menu Pricing & Statistics:** Metric values and pricing utilize Playfair Display numbers for an artisanal bistro feel, accompanied by standard currency symbols set in matching scale.
- **Body & Controls:** Plus Jakarta Sans ensures high legibility on warm bone backgrounds for cut descriptions, weight specs (e.g., `PIEZA 400 G`), and navigation links.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column grid system that emphasizes breathing space, dramatic culinary photography, and unobstructed typography.

- **Grid & Alignments:** Desktop screens (1200px+) employ a 12-column grid with a maximum container width of 1360px, 1.5rem gutters, and 2.5rem outer canvas margins. Tablet viewports (768px–1199px) adapt to 8 columns with 1.25rem gutters. Mobile devices (<768px) collapse to 4 columns with 1rem gutters and 1.25rem margins.
- **Rhythm & White Space:** Generous vertical section spacing (`4rem` to `6rem`) frames every dish as an artisanal showpiece. Dish presentations balance 50/50 horizontal splits between high-saturation food photography and crisp informational text cards.
- **Continuous Marquee Ticker:** A persistent brand ribbon with warm cream background (`#F5EFE6`), terracotta flame dividers, and italicized artisanal credentials (`Maduración propia`, `Brasa de encina`, `Corte a cuchillo`) punctuates transition zones between full-bleed imagery and menu listings.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through tactile surface contrasts, structural framing, and soft ambient warmth rather than synthetic drop shadows:

- **Tonal Canvas Layering:** The base application background rests on Warm Bone (`#FBF7F2`). Secondary product containers and feature panels step forward using Oyster Cream (`#F5EFE6`) or Deep Charcoal Ember (`#14100E`).
- **Low-Contrast Hairline Outlines:** Cards and dialog containers employ delicate 1px boundaries in `#E6DFD5` on light surfaces or `#29211D` on charcoal surfaces, establishing clear edges without harsh separation.
- **Ambient Fire Glow:** Hero segments set on charcoal grounds incorporate subtle, radial warm light gradients centered around the photography (e.g., `radial-gradient(circle at center, rgba(212, 155, 83, 0.15) 0%, rgba(20, 16, 14, 0) 70%)`), imitating live grill embers.
- **Interactive Depth:** Elevated items (e.g., dish showcase cards on hover) lift with a slight translateY (-2px) and an organic warm shadow: `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(20, 16, 14, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a dual geometric language: **architectural precision** for containers, balanced by **refined pill geometries** for interactive triggers and status indicators.

- **Outer Shells & Cards:** Dish display cards, featured cuts, and reservation modules maintain subtle, tailored corner radii (`8px` to `12px`), preserving the tailored structure of an upscale print menu.
- **Interactive Elements & Tags:** All primary action buttons, category selectors, badges (e.g., `LA CASA`), and navigation links adhere to a full pill-shaped language (`border-radius: 9999px`). This creates an inviting touchpoint contrast against rectangular photography blocks.
- **Motif Shapes:** Flame icons are encapsulated in round pill medallions, reinforcing culinary heritage across navigation bars and section anchors.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Reservar / Pedir):** Pill-shaped container (`border-radius: 9999px`) filled with Terracotta Rust (`#9C382C`), crisp white text in `label-action`, horizontal padding of `1.75rem`, vertical padding of `0.75rem`. On hover: shifts to Ember Flame (`#B83E2D`) with subtle scaling. Optional leading flame icon in warm bone.
- **Secondary Action (Ver carta / Contacto):** Pill-shaped with a 1px border in `#14100E` on bone surfaces, or `#E6DFD5` on dark backgrounds. Charcoal text with trailing directional arrows (`→`).
- **Icon Action Buttons:** Circular buttons (`44px × 44px`) housing subtle SVG icons (phone, cutlery, map pin) with 1px hairline border.

### Badges & Chips
- **Curated Badges (e.g., "LA CASA"):** Compact pill badge positioned top-left over food photography. Solid charcoal (`#14100E`) background with warm bone text or terracotta red background with white text. Typography set in uppercase `kicker-eyebrow` (10px).
- **Category Filter Chips:** Pill tags with soft cream background (`#F5EFE6`) and hairline borders (`#E6DFD5`). Selected state transitions to deep charcoal background with bone text.

### Dish Showcase Cards
- Split layout featuring 1:1 or 4:3 high-resolution food photography on the left with rounded corners (`12px`) and product dossier on the right.
- **Top Label:** Cut weight or preparation method in uppercase sand-toned tracking (`PIEZA 400 G`).
- **Title:** `headline-md` in Playfair Display.
- **Description:** `body-md` detailing fire wood type, dry-age duration, and culinary finish.
- **Footer Rule:** Hairline horizontal divider (`#E6DFD5`) separating body from price and inline reservation action.
- **Pricing:** Prominent `label-price` in terracotta or dark charcoal alongside an embedded primary pill trigger (`Reservar y probarla`).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Reservation Fields:** Inputs set on warm bone backgrounds with 1px border in `#E6DFD5`. Focus state illuminates the border in `#9C382C` with zero synthetic glow, maintaining clean architectural lines. Floating labels set in Plus Jakarta Sans.
- **Party Size & Time Pickers:** Segmented horizontal pill controls with smooth toggle transitions.

### Lists & Menu Tickers
- **Credential Marquee:** Continuous edge-to-edge horizontal ticker with warm oyster background (`#F5EFE6`), separated by small terracotta flame SVGs. Typography in italicized Playfair Display or tracked Plus Jakarta Sans.
- **Metric Grids:** 4-column quantitative proof panels (e.g., `1996 - AÑO DE APERTURA`, `40+ DÍAS DE MADURACIÓN`) with numbers in large Playfair Display and descriptors in small uppercase sans-serif.
